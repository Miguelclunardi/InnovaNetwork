UEs and their slices

      

      
Each cell is the PDU session of the UE in that slice (IP).
        Under it, the traffic of the UE in that slice: exact for simulated UEs (counters of the UE TUN of the
        session); for a real UE, its radio rate when it is in that slice only. For a real UE in two slices (the radio
        measures the UE, not the session), its part of each slice is the slice total minus the other UEs in it:
        exact when the others are simulated or idle, an estimate (≈) otherwise.

    
Refused session requests

    
Traffic per slice is read on the UPF tunnel of each slice (one DNN per slice: ogstun,
      ogstun2…), so it is exact per slice. The slice list is read from the running core (AMF/UPF configuration, every 30 s).

UERANSIM (simulated gNB and UEs) 

          

            
            
            
          

          

        

        
Recent actions
    
      
    
    
    
    
    The buttons act on the active core (Open5GS) and its UERANSIM. Stop = scale 0
      (nothing is deleted). Restart recreates the pod and the ones that depend on it (UPF → SMF; AMF → gNB and simulated UEs). "Start all"
      brings everything up in order: MongoDB → NRF → others → UPF → SMF → AMF → UERANSIM gNB → UEs, checking the fiber on AMF/UPF, PFCP and
      NG Setup; a pod that comes up without the fiber is recreated automatically. Switching cores stops the OCUDU gNB and the current core, brings the other one up
      and restarts the gNB with the file that points to the new AMF.
